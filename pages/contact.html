FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our car rental service.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

For a real rental, ask the provider which driving licence and identity documents they accept. This demo does not collect or verify documents.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

You can change dates and times in this demo before confirming. It does not create a real reservation; a real provider’s cancellation policy must be checked separately.

Is there a mileage limit?

Mileage allowances depend on the rental provider and vehicle. The sample daily rate here is for demonstrating the booking interface.

What payment methods do you accept?

The demo does not process a deposit or payment. Your summary shows only the sample daily rate multiplied by rental days.

Do you offer airport pickup and drop-off?

Yes. This website supports guest demo bookings. Choose a car, add your trip details, and review the summary without creating an account.

Rental Guide

1. Choose your car

Browse the cars, compare daily rates, and open View details to check the vehicle.

2. Enter your rental details

Click Rent Now. Choose your pickup city, dates and times, then enter your contact information.

3. Review and confirm

Review the total, accept the rental terms, and confirm your demo booking. You can download a trip summary.

Bring your driving licence and identification for a real rental. This classroom demo does not reserve vehicles or take payments.

DriveEase is a student frontend project. Vehicles, daily rates, pickup cities and availability are illustrative.

The demo total is the daily rate multiplied by rental days. Each started 24-hour period counts as one day. No taxes, insurance charges or deposits are added.

Pickup must be today or later, and return must be after pickup. For a real rental, confirm eligibility, insurance, deposits, fuel policy and cancellation conditions with the provider.

Confirming here creates a demo summary. It does not reserve a vehicle or charge a payment.

Booking and contact entries stay in the current page. They are not submitted to a server or saved in browser storage.

The downloaded trip summary contains only the trip, price and reference. Your theme preference is saved in localStorage.

Vehicle photos are served locally. The Google map on the Contact page may use Google's cookies and network services.
